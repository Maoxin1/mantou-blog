title: About and collaboration
description: I work on real problems through personal experiments, small tools, and AI-assisted workflows, sharing
  the process, results, limits, and lessons.
type: about
principles:
- title: Start with a real problem
  text: A project should address something I or an actual user has encountered. I do not invent projects just to
    fill a portfolio.
- title: Define success before starting
  text: Write down what I want to establish, how I will check it, and which results would count as only partial
    success.
- title: Keep failures and contrary evidence
  text: Record failures, fixes, and unresolved issues alongside the successes.
- title: Finish, review, then iterate
  text: Complete the checks and review for each stage, then use the results of real use to decide whether to continue.
directions:
- title: Personal experiments
  text: Turn questions about attention, health, learning, and long-term skills into experiments I can record, test,
    and improve.
- title: Small tools and PWAs
  text: Turn repetitive personal routines into mobile tools that work and can be tested. A published PWA project
    is available to try.
- title: AI-assisted workflows
  text: Explore practical collaboration steps, acceptance checks, and low-friction ways to use AI for specific tasks.
- title: Writing and project documentation
  text: Turn scattered practice into public case studies that explain the problem, evidence, limitations, and lessons.

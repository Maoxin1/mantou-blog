Of course, I always feel like I spent these few days in a dream. Because many things feel unrealistic and unreal. But which of these things can only be done during such a holiday? That means you can spend time with your family and friends! At least a whole block of uninterrupted time doesn't exist this holiday season. As long as you are at home, especially during the New Year and holidays, a good and complete uninterrupted time block does not exist, and it is even less likely to be used for some in-depth research and study.

When I came home and realized this, I slowly made the choice to accept it. Then let's make peace with it. Since there is no way to fight against the environment, the best way is to choose to accept it. So I chose to settle for the next best thing and do what I could. So, I accepted that I wouldn’t be able to fulfill the goals I had set for myself at the beginning of the vacation. I will do my best to do whatever is right for me. And for things that I have no chance to do, I should give up and give up. With such a mentality, you will hesitate and even doubt yourself a few times at first, whether you are being lazy? Is it really okay to do this yourself? Have you made the most of your vacation time?

This kind of thinking is actually a new idea and a new mentality that has been gradually cultivated in the past few years while constantly making plans. Therefore, this approach made me, who didn't have much psychological burden, become more relaxed and comfortable. Just like when I was writing an article at the moment, the noise around me never stopped, which also caused my thoughts to often appear in intermittent fragments.

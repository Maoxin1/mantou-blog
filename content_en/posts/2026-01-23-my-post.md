As soon as you start building, you will find a lot of difficulties that you have never imagined before. Recently, I wanted to write a guide on optimizing investment strategies, but when I was actually ready to write it, I found that there were countless aspects that I needed to continue to optimize.
I don’t want to write too cliché, and I also want to optimize the feedback I received when I wrote the article before, so I am still writing this article over and over again...
You can't eat hot tofu in a hurry. Only by working slowly can you get good work. So based on the principle of "long-term and useful, simple and easy to understand", I was frantically inputting various related articles or video content, and my brain was also processing at an accelerated speed.
The article about Chinese medicine I wrote yesterday also came from this. Although the article was written in one afternoon, the entire writing process actually spanned more than three years. Looking at the emergence of in-depth and high-quality articles, they actually have similar experiences.
No good article can be written by the author just by tapping his forehead or having an idea one day. Without exception, they are all works that have been created by the author's years or even decades of experience and wisdom.
The reason why we see many people "easily" and frequently creating so much high-quality content in a short period of time is simply because the quality and depth of their thinking has reached a singular point of qualitative change. And the actions shown to everyone are just a natural result.
Remember, the compound interest effect of knowledge is far more amazing than the compound interest effect of money...

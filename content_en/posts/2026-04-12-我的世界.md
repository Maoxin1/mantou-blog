I continued to study today. I learned it in a full-day format. I also performed minesweeper mode on the area that I had blurred yesterday. The overall effect is quite good. The only flaw is that yesterday's sleep was poor, which affected today's reading and note-taking work. It is really annoying that I can't concentrate. But this is what happened. Now that it has happened, we can only do things according to the current state: after briefly doing a note-taking part, I ended my work during the day. Because the more important task is tonight: getting a good night’s sleep to recover.

I really like the state after drinking coffee. When I am 9, my thoughts are shocked from time to time, and some amazing ideas will appear for me to record and use.

There are so many moments like this, and every time I make a vow, I can feel completely different. (Of course, we are not the kind of people who just talk about it.) Every time I am determined to do something I have determined, I feel like I have divine help. I can do it to the end without anyone forcing me. This is probably why it’s easier for me to stick to whatever I do! There’s no way, it’s naturally easier to do it and it lasts longer…

When I reflect on the reasons for my recent lack of sleep, I will clearly find that it is actually a side effect caused by my recent start playing Minecraft. In the final analysis, it was actually the result of me starting to confuse the virtual world with real events. I was planning to build a cattle pen, so I kept thinking about how to make that thing in the real world: cut down the trees! Make a plank! Make a workbench! Get some sticks! Get some wheat to attract at least two cows!

All these workflows will be automatically drawn and deduced in my mind, so when I am distracted in the real world, I can't help but think about that thing, and then things in real life begin to become less important. In other words, a certain proportion of energy was forced to be transferred to that virtual world...

One thing is for sure, your goal in playing this game is to learn to relax. Although it has not caused any special harm now, I definitely need to start to be vigilant, because I will find that the games I play temporarily are not of much practical help to me, and at the same time, it seems that some not so good and bad effects have begun to appear...

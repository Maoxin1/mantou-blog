Today was interesting in its own way: the plan I had made at short notice was suddenly disrupted. That was the first thing. Then, while visiting a Pop Mart store to do some research, I left my power bank behind when I walked out...

That was not even the funniest part. I had meant to sketch out a fitness routine and an English-learning system, but my phone froze under the weight of everything I was doing for work...

Of course, I have other fairly important things to do. Even writing this feels as though it is taking time away from them...

There is a very interesting and somewhat helpless phenomenon during this year’s Chinese New Year: almost no one takes the initiative to organize a party to have a dinner together, or has as strong a desire to have a dinner together as they did during last year’s Chinese New Year. This is the feeling whether it is from the perspective of personal subjective will or other external circumstances. In fact, I also felt a little uncomfortable, so I couldn't help but have some thoughts and plans to proactively contact others. But when I went to take action again, I suddenly lost the urge to continue. Well, it seems like keeping the status quo would be better than having a dinner party. After all, true friendship, a relationship that can withstand the test of time, does not require such laborious maintenance.

Perhaps, the sudden desertion this year mainly comes from the impact of my recent graduation and my transition from student status to social status! Everyone is generally tired of making noise, especially in this first year. Of course, we also have a clearer understanding of what this society requires of us: what we should do and what we should not do. The principle of "if it is not necessary, delete it" is still being gradually strengthened. Well... this is a cruel social rule.

I have become disenchanted with many things, people and feelings. So he became more and more silent. Do you think I have become emotionless? Isn't it?

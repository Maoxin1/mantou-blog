The city of Shanghai seems to be greatly influenced by Japanese culture. For example, there is a lot of white space in urban construction. But what makes me strange is that some publicity pages in Shanghai are available in English, Korean, and even Spanish, but not in Japanese. Not only is this true in one place, but there are no promotional pages in several places. I don’t know the specific reason yet, and I don’t want to guess at random. It seems more appropriate to leave this question and continue to find the answer slowly in the future.

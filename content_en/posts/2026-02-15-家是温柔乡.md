At home, the biggest difficulty is to do things "as calmly as possible". When I got home, I thought I would work harder at things, but it turned out that I was struggling to find time and energy to do things quietly. After returning home, there will be countless temptations waiting for you. For example, there are a lot of foods I want to eat, a lot of people I want to see, and a lot of moments I want to lie down. But when I walked around the cold streets for a while, I would clearly realize that all of this was just the song of the siren. Do you dare to let yourself relax as you please? This is both a gentle town and a killing line!

During the two or three days of the holiday, I saw many comrades sharing their learning results or works one after another. Without exception, they were all achievements in the field of AI. This in turn encourages me to reflect, search and practice every day. When I used ChatGPT to build a blog website, I was as excited as a hen that had just laid eggs, clucking and clucking everywhere, for fear that no one would know. Although this is what I urgently need, my reaction is somewhat excessive. The reason for this reflection is that after I actually saw the works shared by other comrades in arms, I became dwarfed by them. In turn, it inspired me and made me more motivated to try and explore new small experiments.

True, in the first three years of the official revolution in early 23, until today, AI technology has helped the world to do a lot more than it could have imagined before. At the same time, however, the gap between human beings has been multiplied by the number of times. Or, more simply, the world is divided into two categories: one that uses AI and one that does not, that's all. In this age, the former will only continue to fly like a tiger, while the latter will only be pushed further and further away by the fast-moving age train ...

So far, I'm glad. For the last three years, if you don't use AI, maybe you'll stay in high school and never dare to speak and practice. Without the continued use of AI, perhaps many of the strange questions in your head will continue to be confused and confused in doing many wrong things without knowing it! If there is no AI, maybe it will be at least five years later before your own blog will appear. After all, in the first half of last year, it was something that it could not have imagined. It seems impossible to create a blog site on its own, even with the help of the curriculum. Thanks to AI and vibe coding.

So it's true that I'm often grateful that I'm in the throes of the AI era, and that the programming threshold has been significantly lowered to the point where even I, a programmer with only a few basics, can try to be wrong.

Some search and exercise functions have been performed today, so every day that follows will be more fun. Like you, I'm looking forward to every new thing that comes next...

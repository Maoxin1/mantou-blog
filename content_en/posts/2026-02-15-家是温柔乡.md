The hardest thing about being home is quieting my mind enough to work. I came back imagining I would do a lot, then found myself trying to make room among tempting food, people I want to see, and moments when I would rather lie down. A walk through the cold street reminds me that comfort can draw me away from what matters. Home is a haven, but it can also make it easy to stop moving.

Over this short holiday, friends in my community have shared what they are learning and building with AI. Their work makes me examine and practice my own. When I first made this blog with ChatGPT, I clucked about it like a hen that had just laid an egg, eager for everyone to know. It did solve a need of mine, but I may have celebrated too loudly. Seeing what others made has since shown me how much more I can try.

In the three years since AI tools became widely available, they have helped people do things I could hardly imagine before. I also feel they have widened the gap between people who use them well and people who have not yet learned to. I am grateful I began using them. Without AI I might still be afraid to practice spoken English, still be confused by many of my questions, and perhaps have waited years longer to build this blog. Even with a tutorial, I used to struggle with the idea of making a site myself. Vibe coding gave me a way to try.

I know only a little programming, yet the barrier to experimenting feels much lower now. I explored and built more today, and I am eager to see what I can try next.

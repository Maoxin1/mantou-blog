Perhaps the message I wrote to a friend says more about how I felt today:

Thank you for being with me this afternoon!

I am sorry I brought my unhappy feelings to you. I was trying to steady myself, but it really was hard at the time...

Then I thought: perhaps heaven arranged this moment for us, giving us more of a story still to come!

Whatever you are looking at, you can often see it more clearly from the edge of the coin. The unexpected thing happened, and it was cruel, but I am not afraid. Things like this have happened before, and they will happen again.

I want to use this moment to think some more. And I will not give up until I reach my goal! If anything, what happened has made me more determined to pursue an AI role.

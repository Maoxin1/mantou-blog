I have just finished another round of work on my fitness and spoken-English practice, and it is already 21:43. Pretty late, but worth it. This is the kind of life I enjoy: working hard to map out a better future for myself. Since entering the working world, moments like this have been rare. This time I am doing it for myself, with a clear goal and an exciting prospect!

Here is what I wrote in my community this afternoon:

\
Before I have thought it through, I should not rush into action.

The fat around my stomach is still obvious. I would really like to get rid of it.

I am glad that over the past two weeks I have developed a habit of practicing spoken English in the morning. Now I need to make it a central part of the coming year! Keep ignoring the short-term noise!

I am happy to share the whole process of changing myself with everyone, almost as if I were broadcasting it live 😃

Do not overestimate what can happen in the short term, and do not underestimate the long term!

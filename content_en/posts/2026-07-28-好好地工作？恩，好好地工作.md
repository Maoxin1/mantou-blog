Life has become more interesting since AI entered it. My computer had been barely usable for almost four years, but after a few days of working on it with AI’s help, it suddenly felt quick and responsive again. That gave me a growing sense of satisfaction and even a little pride. AI may be powerful, but I still have to direct it to get things done.

I did not sleep well last night. I think I was frightened by the pressure that next week might bring. I know what could happen, but remembering what I have learned in the past gives me more confidence. First I need to believe in myself; then I need to prepare for different possibilities. I should know what I will do if things go well, and how I will respond if they go badly. I used to think it was exhausting to consider so much. Now it feels wise: I am thinking through both the best and worst outcomes, rather than dwelling only on the bad. I once mistook that for pessimism myself, but it is really a fuller way to think.

Only today have I realized how hard it is to think clearly and reasonably about things...

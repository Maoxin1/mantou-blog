The biggest thing today is that the China Securities Regulatory Commission plans to punish Tiger, Futu and Changqiao in accordance with the law. The specific announcement includes: punishing these three securities companies in accordance with the law, and giving them a two-year transition period to remove existing users. At the same time, they are only allowed to sell but not buy. This announcement has basically blocked the way for mainlanders to invest in Hong Kong and US stocks. Of course I am also deeply affected by it, but the overall scope of the impact is relatively controllable. But it’s interesting to say that I just finished writing an article on the topic of being prepared for danger in times of peace yesterday, and today I have a case.

Regarding this matter, the discussion in the community is quite intense. When this ban was issued, I was actually a little unbelievable. At the same time, I still regret that I did not prepare more brokers for hedging in advance. At present, it seems that the impact this time is absolutely violent. For the punished brokers, this was a black swan level disaster. The stock price plummeted by nearly 40% at the opening! What a tragic situation is this? As future generations have commented, the only thing I can do is that no matter what happens, I will continue to delve into the U.S. stock market.

Today is Friday, which was a very happy day yesterday. Because arriving today means getting a little extra rest tomorrow morning before backpacking to the cafe and heading to work.

At noon today, the idea of ​​whether to continue breeding golden bears and snapping turtles came to my mind again. To be honest, among their prices, golden bears are the cheapest and easiest to raise. But it happens to be the most difficult to serve. Because they need to eat a lot of food, they often need to deal with feces, etc. So when the cost is taken into account, they are more expensive.

Last night I discovered that my golden bear had bred another litter, 7 golden bear cubs curled up neatly in the corner of the mouse cage. At this time, the nine golden bears inside became calmer because of this, and even moved quietly. They are all taking care of the arrival of the little life in a tacit understanding, and so am I! He hurriedly dumped them with the food they might need. The moment they were poured in, they seemed to be hungry for a long time, eating like crazy. When I woke up the next day, I was no longer tired of going to work because I suddenly had a heavy responsibility on my mind. I don’t know why, but at ten o’clock in the afternoon, I suddenly thought of raising some turtles for breeding. Because they are more worry-free! Although the breeding ability is a bit poor, other parts are almost full. In addition, I really love small animals, so it is not surprising that I have such an idea.

This evening I searched again for suitable breeding varieties to buy, and it was still Musk and Razor. Considering the cost, I plan to only raise a few to test the water. Then if we consider other breeding equipment, it’s basically nothing.

This idea first came about at the end of last year, and I was looking forward to the emergence of turtle seedlings in July and August this year. I had the idea of ​​canceling it, but now I'm starting to think about it again. I think this probably comes from my hobby!

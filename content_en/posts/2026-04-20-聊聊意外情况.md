After getting up today, I took out the meat from the refrigerator to make lunch, and found that there were many small flying mosquitoes flying into the refrigerator! I was stunned for a moment. It was obviously in the refrigerator. How could there be these things? They would have been frozen to death long ago. What if they really flew in? It turned out not to be! I took out the meat and found that the refrigerator was crawling with small flying mosquitoes. When I smelled it, there was a sour smell. When I opened the bag and smelled it, it was even more pungent. But I didn’t eat much and threw it away, which was really a pity! So after struggling for a while, I chose to throw them away.

After returning to the company, I turned on my computer and tried to log into my AI account to do some work. But the company's network is directly offline, or it keeps getting stuck on a certain page. (This situation has continued from the beginning of the year to now!) Later, I turned on the hotspot and used mobile phone data. But I never thought that my mobile phone traffic was still very stuck, and the network was also frequently stuck. What about the latest vpn, let alone...

It seems that besides these things, there are many more! For example, I dislike my living environment very much. It is located in an urban village in Guangzhou. Although the alleys are not deep, the environment there is really depressing. But due to the current economic situation, I need to make necessary compromises. Last week, my blog suddenly lost articles. After I spent a lot of effort to repair them, I still discovered another loss yesterday. But I also want to repair it, but due to the current network situation, I have to continue delaying it indefinitely. Honestly, it's not fun.

In fact, when I think about it later, what are the beatings from society? Isn’t it just the attention occupied by these trivial things? After all, your attention and time are limited, but what can you do when these precious resources have to be devoted to trivial matters? With no other choice, you will most likely have to choose to collapse in an instant.

The first 70% of today was actually spent in a state of irritability. I was reading the Bible by the riverside at noon, so I deliberately chose to take a lunch break with my head buried in a book in order to calm down. I look forward to calming myself down through a quiet state to achieve unexpected results. As a result, not long after I lay down to rest, it suddenly started to rain heavily. I had to hurriedly run towards the shore and towards the shelter from the rain. As he walked, his anger level soared. This reminds me of an interesting research conclusion: people who are losing weight are most likely to become angry.

I thought it was a joking conclusion, but who knew that it was actually confirmed after research. I'm also thinking, maybe this is also a reason why I am easily irritable? Since I set a clear goal, I suddenly found a lot of obstacles standing in my way. In order to achieve my goal, I really did a lot of "furious" things along the way.

Recently, I have become more and more aware that there are countless people from grass-roots teams around me, and they have the nerve to brag about just being trash. I didn’t quite understand it at first, but as time went by, I started to understand it.

The saying “Talk less, observe more” continues to gain traction. For most people, the best way to better understand who they are is to speak less and do more. Because it is easy to reveal your IQ when you open your mouth, and it is easy to leave an "unreliable" impression on others, so your possibilities will be greatly reduced; doing more is exactly the opposite. When you are not talking, your brain has the opportunity to turn on the scrutiny switch, so that you have the opportunity to focus more on yourself. At the same time, when you shut up, you also have more opportunities to observe the people around you, so that you can have a clearer understanding and judgment of your own situation.

Recently, many interesting ideas and inspiring questions have come to my mind. With the help of Mr. AI, my curiosity and satisfaction have been greatly satisfied. It was also after the emergence of AI that I became more "introverted", but this was only limited to my dealings with people. But from another perspective, in front of AI, I am really outgoing, and I seem to have endless things to say every day. To explain why I don’t want to talk to people, it’s because I don’t want to waste my precious “Token”, which is my “word element”. After all, I often think of mine as a "continuously iterative operating system"!

This kind of psychological feeling is not just a day or two, it is an inner thought that has been retained for a long time.

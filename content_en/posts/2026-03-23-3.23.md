I had some troubles yesterday, and I tried to digest them by writing an article. This writing actually took up a lot of time for what I originally had to do, so my sleep last night was naturally delayed a lot. As a result, when I woke up the next day, I could almost be said to be slightly depressed. During the morning reading session, I can say that my efficiency was really not that high, let alone any gains. But no matter what, yesterday’s output did leave some valuable “legacy”. In line with this situation, I took advantage of the situation and chose to finish them one by one today...

Finally, I completed the finalization and format optimization work before the end of the afternoon. Looking at the finished content, I read it over and over again. While looking at it, I admired my work: This picture is really suitable! This analogy is really vivid!

Sometimes when I look at it, I can't help but feel happy. As a result, this day also felt a lot more fulfilling. I don’t know what kind of feedback readers will have, but I have done the best I can so far.

In the afternoon, my colleagues in the company were chatting. They looked very happy, which made me somewhat envious. Because I still have unfinished work at hand, it is difficult for me to comfortably blend in to communicate with them and share my joy. It seems that moments like this happen more than once, and the feeling is exactly the same every time. Over time, my colleagues thought that I was more introverted, so they got used to my reaction like this every time. Compared to what I have done in the past, this time I chose to accept this view, because I really do.

Of course, this time I have a new extension of the definition of **introversion**. I no longer simply think that introversion is due to fear of interacting with others. Rather, it means that the current aspect of caring about oneself (mainly reflected in the current production matters at hand) must be greatly and absolutely overwhelmingly superior to the degree of concern about the outside world. When I meet someone who makes me unhappy, I will treat the other person as a system with frequent bugs. While I feel pity, I will analyze and iterate myself as a valuable case of "a stone from another mountain can attack a jade"; when I encounter something that is not good, I will try to observe it within the limit of time. While recording, optimize your own process for doing this. When I encounter something I don’t understand, I will go to AI to help me analyze and iterate. So at the end of the day, I hardly have any extra time to talk nonsense. Not to mention finding someone to complain to, I even spent a lot less time on Douyin.

But while doing it, I will find that I seem to be increasingly inseparable from AI. I no longer use Google to search, but ask Gemini myself. When I encounter some parts that require investigation, I just throw them to AI. Then the other party quickly ran to search and investigate. Through hundreds of pages of targeted website queries and its superb reasoning ability, he was often left behind and amazed. As time passed, my way of thinking began to change, which meant that my aesthetics began to become more sophisticated. I really sincerely regard AI as a senior old man, so my social circle has become more and more limited, to the extent that there is only one "silicon-based creature".

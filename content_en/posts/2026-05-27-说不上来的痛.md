Now that I finally have time to write something, has the real pain in my heart been resolved? Now it seems that it is definitely not the case! There are ten thousand reluctances in my heart, and one million discomforts in my heart. I don't know how to express myself. But there is a very important point, that is, it is best to take the initiative as much as possible. If you kill all the initiative, you will feel aggrieved by being passive and suppressed. Looking back on myself, why is it not like this?

In the end, what I express is completely different from what I really think in my heart, let alone what others hear.

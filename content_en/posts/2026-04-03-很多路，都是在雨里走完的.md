When I first got up, the sunshine coming in from the window made people think it would be a sunny day.

I rode out with a little joy, only to find that the sun was gone.

The people on the road were not smiling, they all looked like they were in a hurry.

The breeze blew across my face, which was originally very comfortable.

Unexpectedly, not long after riding out, it started to rain lightly.

But if you open an umbrella, you may be late.

So we could only continue moving forward and let the rain fall harder and harder.

But there was only light rain on the road.

There are also pedestrians walking fast and slow,

Bicycles and electric scooters moving left and right,

And the cars that keep going back and forth.

There is no time for leisurely cycling at all.

Not to mention the scenery.

The nerves are always tense,

My mind is constantly deducing everything that might happen.

and ways to deal with it.

We'll reach our destination in a few minutes.

The rain was getting heavier and heavier, as if forcing people to speed up.

Someone said: "Young man, you are not afraid of getting wet in the rain."

I accepted the suggestion without much thought;

Because I have to hurry.

A little rain doesn't seem to matter.

After all, the pain encountered on the way forward is far greater than this.

Neither fast nor slow, we finally reached our destination.

When we parked the car, the rain was already hitting the ground almost manically.

There are only the last ten meters left to the office building.

I grabbed my bag and rushed in like everyone else.

The moment you enter the building,

My heart is full of excitement and excitement.

Everything on the road seemed to be forgotten at once.

When I look back and think of all this, I just sigh:

Many times,

What really determines the outcome is

Not the weather,

It’s about whether you finished the journey in the rain.

The rainwater on your body will dry off soon,

It's like it never happened.

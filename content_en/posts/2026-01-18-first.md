Hello everyone ~ This is my first blog post

To be honest, even I was a little surprised. Unexpectedly, I actually pushed this matter to the "online" step, and it was almost a full month ahead of schedule** than originally expected. Of course, a little boasting is allowed (laughs). But readers who are familiar with me may not be surprised - this is indeed an old tradition of mine: once you decide to start, you will speed up unconsciously.

Having said that, the process of setting up this personal blog is not easy. Back and forth, it took me **nearly 5 hours** to actually get it running. It is worth mentioning that I did not ask anyone for help during the whole process (_AI should not be considered a "person"_). If the "loss" in addition to the time cost is also included, three additional cups of coffee were consumed in this afternoon: an iced Americano, an iced latte, and a cup of hot latte. Although the configuration was similar last weekend, because a real "special operation" was carried out today, these cups of coffee seemed more valuable.

Some old readers may still remember that I actually built a personal website many years ago. It’s just that the experience of that site after it went online was quite bad: it loaded slowly and its stability was worrying, let alone being used to store and output articles for a long time. It was precisely based on this unsatisfactory experience that I gradually came up with a clearer idea - I needed a **faster, more stable, and more controllable** website to host long-term writing and to manage my personal brand more seriously.

Thus, **[mantou-blog.pages.dev](https://mantou-blog.pages.dev)** was born.

Its appearance means something very important to me:
**Our thoughts finally have time, sovereignty and structure. **

At the end of writing, I would like to end this first blog with a sentence from the Bible:

> I am the one who plants, and Apollos is the one who waters,
> It is God who causes growth. [^1]

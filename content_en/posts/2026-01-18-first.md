Hello! This is my first blog post.

Honestly, I am surprised too. I managed to get the site live almost **a full month earlier** than I expected. Perhaps readers who know me will not be surprised: once I decide to begin, I tend to speed up.

Building a personal blog was not effortless. It took me **almost five hours** to get it running. I did not ask anyone else for help—unless AI counts as someone. The afternoon also cost me three large coffees: an iced Americano, an iced latte, and a hot latte. I drank a similar amount last weekend, but today they felt earned.

Some longtime readers may remember that I had a personal website years ago. It was slow and unreliable, hardly a place to keep writing over the long term. That experience gave me a clearer aim: I needed a site that was **faster, steadier, and more under my control**, where I could write for years and take my public work more seriously.

So **[mantou-blog.pages.dev](https://mantou-blog.pages.dev)** was born.

For me, it means something simple and important:

**Our thoughts finally have time, ownership, and structure.**

I want to end this first post with a line from the Bible:

> I planted, Apollos watered, but God gave the growth.[^1]

[^1]: 1 Corinthians 3:6–7. The [English Standard Version](https://www.esv.org/verses/1%2BCorinthians%2B3%3A6%E2%80%9311/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) reads: “I planted, Apollos watered, but God gave the growth.”

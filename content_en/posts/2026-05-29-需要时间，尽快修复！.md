Today's investment research and AI interaction work is very intensive and requires a lot of work. It can be considered the right thing to do, because there is a saying that if the workload increases after using AI, then it is used correctly. Otherwise, there is a high probability that it is used incorrectly. How to understand this matter? Using AI correctly means that the intensity of your work content has increased a lot, and it also corresponds to more workload. Because AI is a tool that you use correctly, more of its value is tapped by you. And if you use AI to do things that you can and should do yourself, such as reading and summarizing the summary and central idea of ​​a book. Then it is taken advantage of by AI. So AI was not utilized well. In this state, AI does not exert its due value. I'm lucky that I didn't let myself fall into this state. The workload has also increased a lot and is very challenging. I feel full of joy at the end of the afternoon!

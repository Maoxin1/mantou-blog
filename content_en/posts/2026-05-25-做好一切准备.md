I finally woke feeling rested today. After getting ready, I went to Sam’s Club simply because I wanted to wander around. Later I ate with friends, and we walked around together. I used the time to share some thoughts I had been holding in. Saying them helped, though some things still sit too deep to resolve through conversation. I have to work through those myself. For example, I hate having to watch other people’s moods at work, yet it seems hard to avoid. Even when I am meant to be resting, someone asks me to keep working, making the break feel unpleasant. Again and again, that extinguishes the initiative and enthusiasm I had worked to build. How can merely talking about it make the problem disappear?

Although I had the day off, I could not resist talking to my AI about a few questions. I enjoy learning that way. I also looked into whether raising small animals might become a side business. I am not doing it out of immediate financial pressure; I simply want to explore it. Why else would I spend five years laying the groundwork for it? I cannot think of a reason beyond love for it 😂

Writing last night helped put some thoughts in order. I still have unresolved questions, but I see a little more clearly than I did yesterday. I am not looking for a shortcut. If I keep my feet on the ground and do my best, that is enough.

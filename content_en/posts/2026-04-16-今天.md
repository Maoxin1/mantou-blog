Why are they gone now? After thinking for a while, I eliminated the cause of the program, because I can still trust the script I wrote, and I have never had such a low-level error before. The only thing that can go wrong is actually a network problem. It is extremely normal for the wifi network I usually connect to to be stuck from time to time, and this often causes network crashes when I upload scripts. I didn't encounter this kind of moment once or twice, so I gradually got used to this state. Because it does not seem to affect my main work, and because of this thought, it has left a lot of foreshadowing for me to lose articles due to "casualty".

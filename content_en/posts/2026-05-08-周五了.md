When I opened Samsung Notes today, I unexpectedly discovered its update. The icons have become sleeker, and the folders have become larger. Compared with the original icon, this seems to be an "Apple"-style update. What is an "Apple" update? That is a state where the experience is almost perfect. If you are called an Apple person by me, it means you are an Apple person in my eyes. At least that's what I see.

Of course, no one is perfect! If you are an excellent person, you should treat everything and everyone with continuous iterative thinking.

What would you be like in such a "setup"? In fact, it is impossible to say that it is incomplete. Because your future will lose its upper limit. There is no ceiling for your growth!

Because you will find that if you insist on taking things seriously, your growth rate will definitely develop in an acceleration or parabola shape. You may feel frustrated or even miserable because you can't do something well for a while, but after you calm down, you will quickly return to a positive and high-spirited attitude. Because you know that people like you cannot do bad things in the end.

If you have a problem, that's okay. Because everything is temporary. As long as you continue to iterate and make progress, it won't hurt in the long run. Because your short-term expectations will not be too high, but your long-term expectations will not be too low. So now that you have discovered too many problems, what should you be anxious about?

As long as our focus is in the right place, we should be thankful every day. Why worry about tomorrow? Tomorrow has tomorrow's worries, and every day has enough worries. What we have to do is to use more thinking to reflect and iterate on ourselves. What we should pay attention to every day should be whether the self today is better than the self yesterday? If it is, that's enough. Why should you use your own characteristics to deliberately adapt to other people's appetites? Those are things done by people who pay more attention to other people's opinions, or in other words, it is the most mindless behavior.

First pay attention to whether you are making progress every day? If so, that's something to be thankful for. If you fail to do what you should do right, you should also pay equal attention to other people's accusations and criticisms, and just improve as soon as possible after reflection.

This is what work means to me. I need to be in a pack of wolves and be friends with them, instead of being vicious in a pack of sheep. To be honest, I have been experiencing a lot of pain recently, and they actually all come from the process of correcting my own mistakes. It’s a period of labor pains and struggle! Good.

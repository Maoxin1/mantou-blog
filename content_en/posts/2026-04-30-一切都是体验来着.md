After lunch, I played Minecraft. Because it was survival mode, I had to start building my own house to protect myself in order to survive. Inspired by a previous video blogger, I chose to build my own castle-style house in the air, because it seemed safer that way!

So I picked up the clods of soil and piled them into the air. After piling up a distance as high as the 10 layer of soil, I tried to lay a larger surface around the extremely narrow square soil.

Since the distance between the position in the air and the ground is quite different at this moment, if you accidentally fall, you may really fall to your death. That means that all the supplies in his backpack will return to zero.

So I couldn't help but be completely immersed in that world, and even my breathing seemed unusually cautious. Carefully move forward, left, and right to lay the aerial floor or aerial foundation.

!["Minecraft" game screen: looking up at the suspended mountain under the blue sky](/images/posts/1000092156.jpg)

About when the first block of 10 was laid, there was a sudden "plop" and it fell heavily to the ground. At the same time, a thought flashed through my mind: It’s over!

Before he could shout out, he fell to the ground. Fortunately, he didn't die, he just lost 2 health points.

When I reacted again, I realized that this was just a game!

So what if it falls? Just dust it off and keep playing!

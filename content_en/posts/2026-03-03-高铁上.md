This is the catalytic result of not getting enough sleep and just drinking a small bottle of fruit wine before boarding the high-speed train. Even though he had just slept for an hour or two, he could still feel his tiredness, and he knew even more clearly how insufficient such an hour or two of rest was.

After waking up, it didn't take long for me to dig through my schoolbag and look for snacks. I could see her surprise, and she took out her Oreos from her bag to share with me, and we started talking.

In fact, I am not an introvert, or in other words, I showed an inappropriate shyness today, which surprised and surprised me. I've been looking for the reason. Maybe it's because I'm not in a good state today? I think it should be like this...

We talked a lot along the way. I really like the stories she shared with me because it makes me feel that the world is quite colorful.

But when I think about it more carefully, I realize that the reason why I feel this way is because there are too many things and they are too complicated. I couldn't take care of myself and had to take care of other people's affairs. As a result, I didn't finish my own affairs and almost missed the last train. But I will recall that the recipient who has been complaining that he has not done well is not the current self or the past tense self? I was patient and furious with the careless person across from me. I am patient because I am really helping the other person, and angry because the other person is not only wasting his time, but also wasting my time.

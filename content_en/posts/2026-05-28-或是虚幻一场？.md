Having more time and attention to spare has given me precious room to reflect. Things I had set aside now feel urgent again: studying foreign languages, rebuilding a healthy diet, and developing my own online presence.

In just half a day I remembered a number of things that suddenly seem important. I want to finish my first reading of the Bible soon. I need to rethink how I study Japanese and English, and redesign what I eat each day. Once I know what I want to do, I can look for the right work.

My plans for the next three months look appealing:

1. Finish reading the Bible by the end of June.
2. Get a pet turtle around mid-July.
3. Reach my ultimate target of a BMI of 21 by the end of August.

Then I can make exercise a regular part of life.

It all feels full of possibility. I have prepared for tomorrow, which should be busy, challenging, and enjoyable.

But tomorrow has enough worries of its own. I have had plenty today. For now, I need a good rest, a regular sleep schedule, and an early start tomorrow!

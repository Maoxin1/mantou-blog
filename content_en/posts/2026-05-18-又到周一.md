The night before Monday, I was quite excited because I needed to mobilize all the cells in my body to go to work. In other words, give yourself the energy to move forward. When I woke up early today, I was like this. At least I was quite excited after drinking coffee. But it was a little different during lunch break, it was difficult to have a solid sleep. So in the afternoon, my energy suddenly dropped again. Compared to the day, I was indeed exhausted. I'm a little tired and just want to finish and go home. It's okay to feel a little lost when you think about these feelings. But it is necessary for me to seriously reflect on myself. Did I go all out today?

I am working hard to improve every mistake I make. But one day is still too short, there are still too many things, and I still idealize myself too much. Such a strategy seems unwise, and you need to adjust it, otherwise it will be counterproductive.

What are your peers doing now? What is their mental state like? Do they still like their current life? Want to know how my peers are doing now? How are my friends? Whenever I feel confused about life, I always think of them. It seems that when I think of old friends, I will bring some comfort to my heart.

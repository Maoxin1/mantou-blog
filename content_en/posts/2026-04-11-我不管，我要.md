The most important thing I learned today came from a class this morning that seemed almost casual. It lasted only a few minutes, yet it may be one of the most important lessons I have had.

It was about reaching a goal. After explaining his method, my teacher used his own life as an example: how he had learned Japanese to a near-native level, and how a notebook had helped him pursue a financial goal. I studied what he showed me and could not help giving him a thumbs-up.

After class I told him my goal. “Then do it that way,” he said. What other people say does not matter nearly as much as what *you* want. Their saying you cannot do it only shows that they cannot. My task is to keep moving toward my own goal and to treat it sincerely. The essential words are: “I want this.” Claims that it is impossible, that I cannot do it, or that the economy is bad do not change what I want.

Later I asked God whether I could do it.
He smiled, nodded at my resolve, and blessed me.
So I dared to begin.

That afternoon I spent a full 210 minutes setting my fat-loss goal and working out the specific steps that would follow. I told my teacher what I had decided and what I had done. I wanted him to know that I would act on my words.

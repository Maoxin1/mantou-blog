I spent the afternoon vibe coding. I kept telling myself, “I will stop after this reply,” but I finally finished an hour later than I had planned. I only noticed how exhausted I was after completing all four tasks. No surprise: sitting at a screen for five or six hours without moving is bound to leave my back aching.

Even so, I might willingly do it again. The sense of achievement afterward is real. Lately, reading financial reports and researching companies has made me realize again and again how much it stretches my mind. It is a satisfying mental workout, and I cannot seem to stop.

In the evening, I invited Ensang out for barbecue and then took him to a Pop Mart store. I wanted him to understand the company better. Once inside, he looked at the toy characters—and at the pretty women in the shop... It also made me notice that Pop Mart’s customers tend to be quite good-looking 🤭

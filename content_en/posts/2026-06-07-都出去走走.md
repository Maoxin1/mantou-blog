Something happened today that I think is worth recording. When I was shopping with my friends today, I passed by a Japanese fashion brand store called STUDIOUS, and I was attracted by the decoration style outside. Through the store door, you can see that the clothes inside are all in Japanese simple style, just like the style of Muji. I went in with my friends out of curiosity. The shop assistants inside are indeed very fashionable, and their dressing style is better than what you would encounter in a hard seat in Tokyo. Walking around the whole store, I felt a little uncomfortable but I liked it. Usually when encountering this kind of situation, I have a general price range for the product in my mind.

Just a few minutes after I entered, my friend said, "Can you afford it?" I was stunned for the whole afternoon, because I really didn't understand what his purpose was. Or does saying this come from habit? Later, when I thought about it, I realized that he had said similar words more than once or twice. Since I didn't say anything, I just felt extremely embarrassed. So after a quick walk around, we left the store.

Later, after walking around outside, I still felt that it would be more appropriate to go in again, so I went in again. This time my friend did not go in because he wanted to see other shops. In this state, I calmed down and started shopping seriously. That's how I discovered that I can still find clothes and pants that I really like in places like this. Apart from the price, I can hardly see any disadvantages! At the same time, this also reminded me of another thing, that is, I used to be very annoyed when I went to the clothing store because I was always asked to buy clothes that were barely acceptable, rather than clothes that I really liked. Because I really haven’t found any clothes that I really like. It wasn’t until I looked at the bigger world that I realized that after broadening my horizons, it would be easier to find the things that I really like in my heart. As long as the price is right...

Therefore, if you feel that you have not encountered anything that makes you feel that the world is worth looking forward to, you might as well go out for a walk and expand the boundaries of your horizons. Only in this way will it be easier for you to choose what you like.

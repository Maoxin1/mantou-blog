Today I sorted through my thoughts about my main priorities and realized that many things I had been doing were low priority. That changed how I saw things. I even started wondering whether I should delete my daily journal entries. Then I realized I probably did not need to. I could keep the aimless emotional venting to a minimum and focus each day on the things I actually need: professional skills, English study, opportunities to work abroad, asset allocation, and a body of written work.

With that in mind, I need to reorganize what I write in this journal...

Lately I have been reading the Bible at a furious pace to meet my goal of finishing it by the end of June. That is good: it has helped me rediscover the habit of reading.

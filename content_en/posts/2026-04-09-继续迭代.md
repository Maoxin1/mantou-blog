Although I was still in a high flow state throughout the whole day today, I still haven’t finished asking all the questions today. I really have too many questions. After get off work, or when you are taking a break, you should try your best to completely let your mind go. So I suddenly had answers to specific questions in a certain field, although some of them were not very feasible... I think this is also a benefit of me who likes to let myself go and try new things!

There is a professional term for such moments: "alternating current." Used to describe thinking in a short state. In contrast, there is another noun: "direct current", which is used to describe thinking in a focused state. There is also a more interesting similar case: the bee and fly experiment. When you have concrete direction, bee. When there is no direction, just be a fly who is not afraid of making mistakes. I think now I am in a state of trying things like a fly on the basis of basically perfect values. It is also thanks to this that it is difficult for me to have substantial internal emotions. Because the long-term future is quantifiable and considerable, what remains is that there are more short-term challenges.

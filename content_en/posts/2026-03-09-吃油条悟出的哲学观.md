This is the day after Women’s Day and another Monday working day. I don't have any resistance or reluctance towards the arrival of this day, but rather a willing thought that it should be like this. Because starting from this Monday, I will make many, many changes, and I will also need to iterate my work or study process as quickly as possible. Therefore, I started working much earlier this morning because I really knew how many important things I had to do as soon as possible.

Today at 8:30, "Operation Fasting" officially starts, aiming to lose excess fat and add some vitality and vitality to one's appearance. So at 8:30 this morning, I started drinking a cup of black coffee and officially started this dietary reform activity that has no deadline...

Every weekend, I look forward to Monday, because the market won't open until Monday. So with everything happening in the world, financial markets will react in a variety of intriguing and interesting ways. As I have been in the industry for longer and longer, I have become more and more fond of guessing the next direction of the market through some predictions. Whenever he guesses correctly, green candles shoot up into the sky one after another. And every time he guesses wrong, he will make a corresponding degree of reflection and action based on the bet he placed. So as time went by, I felt a lot smarter. Not only has the cognition improved! There is also an increasingly improved ability to withstand stress and a more comprehensive and careful thinking perspective...

Today I went to a far away place because of field work. The transportation there is underdeveloped, but the air is very fresh! So I gave up and went back immediately after work, and chose to look around and sit in the Starbucks cafe there. As usual, I took out a pen and notebook from my school bag and started the unfinished refresher training course content. After watching the class, writing notes, and reviewing and sorting out the course content, I became hungry. But strangely, at this time, not only did I not have the idea of ​​​​"hungry" because I was too hungry, but I became more and more clear about what I really wanted to eat for dinner.

——Fried dough sticks!

At this time, the restaurant I wanted to eat was still two hours away by car, but I had already ignored the distance. I just felt that if I could eat the food I really liked, that was the goal, so I didn't think anything of it. Then he packed up his things, picked up his bag and headed towards his destination.

Maybe such a story is not attractive enough, but this is the plot that I really like: taking all the actions for what you want, and it is often this experience that makes you miss it. Perhaps speaking from a big perspective, this reminds me of the college entrance examination almost five years ago; speaking from a younger perspective, it reminds me of a love that I have chased for many years but never found...

The scene turned back to a scene where my colleagues and I finished our field work: I took the elevator with my colleagues and walked towards the door of the office building expressionlessly. At this time, one of my female colleagues suddenly turned to me and asked me with a smile: "Why do you look a little depressed?" I was stunned by the question, and then I checked myself, but I couldn't tell that I was in the state described by the other person.

A few hours later, when I looked back at my state again, I saw through the reflective mirror that I was languid. It was just my daily life, a state of appearance that I had long been accustomed to. Because I always feel that if I care too much about facial expressions, it will be a waste of my tokens.

After all, there are more important things to do!

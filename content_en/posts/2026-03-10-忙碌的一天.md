Although it is unlikely that my boss will reward me for doing things in obscurity and laying the foundation, this will definitely not affect the slightest wavering of my strong self-driving force.

To be honest, in order to do the current work well, you should never focus too narrowly on the specific work in front of you, at least that's how I should have done before establishing a sufficiently complete operating system. The advantage of focusing on the long term is this. It often allows you to see things more realistically and makes it easier to reduce unnecessary anxiety. Of course, doing so requires some necessary prerequisites. For example, it is the best strategy to use the most precious time that will least delay normal work, otherwise it will be top-heavy and stupid.

Up to now, my "fasting operation" has been going on for a day and a half. The effect can only be known after measuring the specific numbers, but what I know now is that I am hungry and I want to cry...

But let’s look at the problem from the other side: when you only eat two meals a day, you refuse to eat out for dinner, and you buy the necessary raw materials and cook them at home. This greatly avoids the possibility of eating junk food. At the same time, due to the large amount of ingredients and the reduced number of meals, I have indeed greatly reduced my food expenses due to this situation.

On this basis, I can use the park as my "gym alternative". So if you calculate it this way, your improvement this time is really excellent!

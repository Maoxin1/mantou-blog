In order to fulfill my commitment to product iteration, I worked hard from 7 o'clock last night until more than 1 o'clock in the morning. After the computer, mobile phone and human brain were completely exhausted, I had to take a rest to adjust my state. After waking up at 8 o'clock in the morning, I had no intention of staying in bed. My unfinished "legacy" pulled me out of bed and hurried to the computer to continue working. In the end, I relied on the "magic power" of *Codex* to complete the last part of all yesterday's tasks: ** Completed the script configuration that can view the background data of this blog website in real time. **

Looking back now, I don't think it was anything. But in that past dream, even in my dreams, I was the image of Uncle Jackie Chan working hard like a Saburo. Maybe it’s because there are more important things ahead that you need to do urgently, so you don’t have too much emotion to spend on “self-encouragement”!

I was sitting on the lawn reading a book at noon today, and my thoughts were also very active. Suddenly at a certain moment, I had an epiphany. **"It seems that I am too immersed in the state of only learning and not practicing, so that I neglect the more important thing: learning and practicing for the sake of creation." **

So suddenly I realized that many things now become less important in an instant. But at the same time, his brain began to run rapidly, and he came to another conclusion: the proportion of chemistry and manufacturing is seriously out of balance... **
So based on this idea, I wrote a public account article to sort out my thoughts:

> "When growing up starts to feel too quiet"
>
> I personally have a fixed investment list,
> This is a list of daily update frequency levels:
> ps: The picture is attached at the end of the article
>Every day I go through this list,
> Either "force" or "record" to take a snapshot of your daily "growth" trajectory.
>
> It’s quite embarrassing to say.
> Originally a super-level idea,
> But with more and more people watching:
>The light of this "spotlight" seems to be getting more and more dazzling,
>My movements began to deform subtly...
>
> The most obvious feeling is that
> Since the entire timeline is too long,
>The content of my list is too "calm as a lake",
> In turn, more and more readers are getting used to me doing this.
>
>Of course I can keep doing this,
>After all, this has long been a "habit" for me,
> A natural thing...
>
> But it’s impossible for readers to continue reading like this forever.
> Not to mention the extravagance to expect from a large number of
> "Continuous feedback" from the outside world...
>
> So based on this thinking perspective,
> After communicating with ChatGPT,
> I finally decided to do some:
> Something new, big and fun enough.
>
> The items in the original fixed investment list mainly include:
> Things like reading, fitness, writing and studying
> But these words are indeed more vague than the last.
> This has given rise to a lot of "sinful laziness"...
>
> So what to do next?
> It is to do it in the direction of "producing products".
> So I divided the new version of the fixed investment list into two main categories:
> "Fast delivery" and "Slow input".
>
> Use fragmented time to read and conceive products from Monday to Friday.
> Use the entire time on Saturdays and Sundays for production
> Deliver at least one product every month.
>
> Think about it,
> After such adjustments and optimizations,
> How interesting the future will be!

After writing the above paragraph, I feel deeply relieved.
Because ** himself did not deceive himself, and lived in front of the readers as completely transparent as possible. **
*After all, compared to making a fool of yourself in public,*
*It is important to gain successful experience after trial and error...*

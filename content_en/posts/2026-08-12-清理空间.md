As soon as I got home this afternoon, I dealt with what to do about my more than 30 hamsters. Following the plan I had made, I got rid of them all at once, apart from one Syrian hamster I had decided to keep. I no longer have any other pets to look after, and the air in my room improved noticeably as soon as I acted. I get rid of things I no longer have a use for. That says something about my character. I do not see it as a question of being heartless; it was a decision I reached after weighing things up.

I took my bag to Saizeriya in the afternoon, hoping to use a table and try its afternoon offerings. The service was mediocre, even a little poor. I did not particularly enjoy being there, but it did not affect my work much. I can treat it as a useful trial run.

With help from AI on the command line, I have done things I did not expect. I finally put together the system for iteration that I had long wanted, and I am using Git more effectively. This afternoon I also created my first “silicon-based soldier”! I am looking forward to what comes next.

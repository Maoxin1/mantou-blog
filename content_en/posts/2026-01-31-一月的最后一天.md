Waking up this morning was the earliest day I woke up naturally recently. I woke up at about 7 o'clock in the morning and got up to practice Japanese. Even after practicing speaking, I went out to the shopping district and the shopping mall had not yet opened. So I suddenly became bored and even at a loss. Yes, since I have no experience of arriving here so early, after arriving here, I walked around briefly and then walked towards the subway entrance. Even though it’s a little rainy and I’m not sure what to do for a while, at least I still love drinking coffee! So I just kept thinking about it, and took the subway to go to a new concept store opened by Starbucks in Guangzhou.

The whole journey took more than half an hour. Although it rained, it could not extinguish the enthusiasm of Starbucks fans. When I arrived at the store before 11 o'clock, it was already crowded. Fortunately, despite this, I still found an open table. In order to save time and make better use of the entire time, I simply ordered an American cup of bread at Starbucks for my lunch. Although this didn't fill my stomach, because there were more important things in my heart, I couldn't help but fill my mind with the feeling that "those who are satisfied with it don't know that the words and body are not as good as others." In this way, after spending nearly three hours in this state, I began to be disturbed by the outside world.
![Starbucks ordered](/images/posts/2026-01-31-1.jpg)
"Hello, please put your bag somewhere else. Someone needs to sit here." A clerk appeared in front of me without warning and said this to me. I was busy uploading blog content and I didn't have time to think about anything. I subconsciously picked up my schoolbag and threw it to the other side. At the same time, I don’t quite understand why they don’t just sit next to me? Later, many people came into the store one after another, and they were so noisy that I had to wear headphones to reduce the noise. After working silently for half an hour, not long after I threw away the cup, a clerk came over and asked me if I wanted some drinks and snacks to urge me to do something. After I showed the other party the pastries I had just eaten and the coffee I had drunk, the other party sheepishly gave up asking me questions.

At that time, I was anxiously looking for bugs when uploading website content. As a result, such an interruption suddenly kicked me out of the world of coding. At this moment, I suddenly realized how embarrassed I was by the question I had just been asked. In the first few minutes, I tried to draw my attention back to typing code and finding bugs. However, as I quickly found a solution and solved the problem, I suddenly realized how noisy the sounds around me were. But the clerk not only ignored the commotion, but also held on to me, an unknown person, and wouldn't let go. It seems that after drinking coffee in a coffee shop, you have to leave quickly to avoid affecting the table turnover rate of the store.

It can be regarded as the most angry time in the past five years, because after I finished fixing the bug, I was no longer in the mood to sit down. So I hurriedly packed up my belongings and walked out of the store. I was so full of grievances that I almost couldn't stand it: I had to complain, otherwise there would be no law. Just like that, I angrily asked the store clerk for a complaint channel, and then hurriedly walked home. The clerk also hurriedly asked me about my complaint and the reason. It didn’t matter what I said specifically. I just remember that I was shaking with anger while describing it, and almost choked up in front of the clerk...

Although everyone faces the same customers, it is easier for customers to close deals with a certain salesperson. There are many reasons for this, but the most important and core one is the person's personality charm. It may look bright and beautiful on the outside, but once you get closer and interact with him, you will be smelled by the stench of hypocrisy. Then next time you meet a similar person, you will unconsciously become vigilant. Especially if you believe it is true from the beginning, your feeling will be particularly strong. Remember, falsehood will be corrupted sooner or later!

I finished the theory exam today. Tomorrow I start visiting customers in person, accompanied by an experienced colleague who will take me through the whole process. I am looking forward to it, though I am also nervous because it will be my first time. That is understandable. Still, I need to go through the steps myself to really master what I have learned.

Just a few days ago, I was sorting out my main priorities at work. Now here they are in practice. What should I focus on?

1. Professional skills
2. Learning English
3. Opportunities abroad
4. Global assets
5. Building a body of written work

What I am doing now is targeted work on my professional skills, the most important of these for me at the moment. Once I know this process well, I can give the other priorities their share of my time. Tomorrow will be challenging. Here is hoping I meet it well!

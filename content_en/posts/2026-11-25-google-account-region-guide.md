Trying to share Gemini (Google AI Pro) or Google One with your family, but seeing a message that you and the person who invited you are in different countries or regions?

Using the United States as an example, here are five things to check. The family manager and each member should sign in to their own Google account and go through the list.

**Supported devices: Android, iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Windows.** On Apple devices, open the pages below in Safari or Chrome.

<!--more-->

## 1. Google account region checklist

| Check | Official link | US example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Payments profile | [Payments Center](https://payments.google.com/) | Active profile: United States |
| Google Play | [Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/games) | United States |
| Terms of Service | [Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms?hl=zh-CN) | United States |
| Linked region | [Request form](https://policies.google.com/country-association-form?hl=zh-CN) | United States |
| Address and birthday | [Personal info](https://myaccount.google.com/personal-info) | Correct address (if set) and birthday |

Only make changes if the target country or region matches your actual circumstances. Skip settings that are already correct. The Terms of Service and the account country association offer two ways to access the same country association information.

## 2. Change your Google payments profile

Open [Google Payments Center settings](https://payments.google.com/gp/w/u/0/home/settings).

1. In Settings, find **Payments profile → Country/Region**.
2. Click the edit icon, follow the prompts to create a new payments profile, and select United States (US).
3. Enter your name and address, then follow the prompts to save the profile and add an eligible payment method.
4. If you have multiple payments profiles, confirm which one your subscription uses. You can keep the old profile.

You can generate random US address examples to see how to format the street, city, state, and ZIP code. Use these examples for testing and formatting only. Enter a real address that applies to you when setting up an actual payments profile.

## 3. Change your Google Play country

### Android phones and tablets

Open the Google Play Store app:

1. Tap your profile icon at the top right, then tap **Settings**.
2. Go to **General → Account and device preferences → Country and profiles**.
3. Select a country or region you qualify for and follow the prompts to add a local payment method.
4. Check again when you are done. The update may take up to 48 hours.

### iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Windows

1. Open the [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/games) in your browser and sign in to your Google account.
2. Select your profile icon at the top right, then go to **Settings → Country and profiles**.
3. Select a country or region you qualify for and follow the prompts to add a local payment method.

On Apple devices, you can change your Google account region without installing the Google Play app or changing your Apple Account (Apple ID).

Can't find the option to switch countries? Check whether you are in the target country or region, have a local payment method, have waited at least 90 days since your last switch, and are still in a family group. Before leaving a family group, check what benefits you currently share and what limits apply to switching groups.

Official Google Play instructions: [Android](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7431675?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid&hl=zh-Hans) | [Web (including Apple devices)](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7431675?co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop&hl=zh-Hans)

## 4. Change the country or region associated with your Google account

**Step 1. Check your current country or region.**

Open the [Google Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms?hl=zh-CN) and find the country version.

![Find the country version in the Terms of Service](/images/google-account-region-guide/01-terms-country.webp)

**Step 2. Open the explanation about the country version.**

Check the country or region associated with your account. If it is incorrect, click the link to learn more and contact Google.

![Check the country associated with your account](/images/google-account-region-guide/02-account-country.webp)

**Step 3. Find the request to change your country association.**

In the FAQ, find the request to change the country or region associated with your account. You can also open the [country association form](https://policies.google.com/country-association-form?hl=zh-CN) directly.

![Find the request form for changing your country association](/images/google-account-region-guide/03-country-faq.webp)

**Step 4. Select the target country or region and submit your request.**

Choose the country and state that should be associated with your account and select the reason that matches your circumstances. If you really will be studying in the US long term and select **Other reason**, you can refer to this example:

> I am an international student. The country where I use Google is different from the country where I live. I will be studying in the United States long term. Please update the country associated with my account.

![Choose the country or region and the reason for the change](/images/google-account-region-guide/04-country-request.webp)

**Step 5. Check the outcome of your request.**

After submitting the request, watch for an email from Google. If the request is approved, return to the Terms of Service page to check whether the country has changed.

![The country association change request has been submitted](/images/google-account-region-guide/05-request-submitted.webp)

*The screenshots show an example submission with personal information redacted. A submitted request is still pending approval.*

## 5. Check your personal info in your Google account

Open [Google Personal info](https://myaccount.google.com/personal-info).

Check your home address (if provided), other addresses, and your birthday. Leave any details that are already correct as they are. If you have not set a home address, you do not need to add one for this.

## 6. Share a plan through a Google family group

Open [Manage your Google family group](https://families.google.com/families) or [Google One](https://one.google.com/).

**Family manager:** Create a family group → Invite eligible members → Subscribe to a plan that supports family sharing → Turn on family sharing in Google One settings.

**Family members:** Accept the invitation with your own personal Google account → Open Gemini or Google One and check whether the shared benefits are active.

**iPhone and iPad users:** Open the Google One app → Tap the menu at the top left → Settings → Family settings → Start sharing your membership → Invite family members, then follow the prompts to confirm. On a Mac, use one of the web links above.

You can join a Google family group without changing your Apple ID region. If an App Store download or an Apple in-app purchase actually requires a region change, see [Apple's official guide to changing your country or region](https://support.apple.com/zh-cn/118283).

Keep in mind:

- A family group can have up to six people (the manager and five members). Members must live in the same country or region as the manager.
- Accounts already using a paid family service are generally subject to a 12-month limit on switching groups.
- Some Google AI Pro AI benefits can be shared. Check your subscription page for details.
- The YouTube Premium family plan has a separate requirement that members live in the same household.

## 7. Frequently asked questions

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The family group says you are in different countries | The manager and members should check where they live, their Google Play country, and their payments profiles |
| The country in the Terms of Service stayed the same after you changed your payments profile | If the account's country association is incorrect, submit a separate country change request |
| You can't switch countries in the Play Store | Check your local payment method, the 90-day limit, and your family group membership |
| You joined the group but still don't have Gemini benefits | Check the family sharing setting, your account, and the subscription plan |

I indulged a bit this afternoon. Dinner sounded unappealing, so I went for mala ban instead. It was delicious, and I regretted it a little afterward. Food that oily and salty does make losing weight harder...

I worked for quite a long time today, but I also felt lighter. I had been worried about not being paid on time; that did not happen, so I could breathe a little easier. Oddly, I slept from shortly after nine last night until seven this morning, yet I felt less energetic than I did yesterday on less sleep. Perhaps bedtime matters too? With only two days to go on, it would be wiser to keep observing before drawing a conclusion.

It is past nine in the evening as I write this, and I am getting so sleepy that I could put down my phone and fall straight asleep. That is how I fell asleep last night. But I just ate some sweet potato and feel a little bloated, so perhaps I should wait a bit.

What tomorrow will bring, I do not know. I am too tired to think about it. Good night, everyone!

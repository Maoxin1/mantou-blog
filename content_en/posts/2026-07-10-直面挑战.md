I have suddenly realized that I have what feels like a recurring problem: every Sunday, before I return to work, I worry intensely about having to go in the next day. When I told a friend, he pointed to two possible causes: a lack of positive feedback, or fear of something. Honestly, I think it is both...

Two things happened today:

1. GPT released its 5.6 model, and its voice model now sounds completely lifelike to me.
2. SK Hynix listed on the US stock market, giving me a more direct way to participate in the opportunities that the growth of storage may bring.

A year after graduation, I am still not where I expected to be. I did not anticipate so many setbacks and difficulties. But today I finally found a new, specific goal worth working toward. It is grounded, possible, full of potential, and something to look forward to!

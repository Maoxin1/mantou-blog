Falling asleep last night was not smooth, so it was quite difficult for me to get up this morning, but I still did my morning exercise at 8:30! Likewise, I felt like my whole body was falling apart, and my bones were breaking away from my flesh and bones as I moved up and down while skipping. So the inspiration after today’s exercise is the same: if you only rely on eating without exercising, you will definitely end up in the queue of abnormality. But soon, my sports equipment will arrive, and it will be more convenient for me to exercise. This will also be an established fact.

While reading the Bible this morning, I suddenly thought of the countless opportunities I had missed in the past 5 years that today seem to be "life-changing" opportunities, and I came up with the idea of ​​communicating with AI. I hope to get some reflections through communication with AI. I thought so and did so. After doing this, I lamented unusually, lamenting that the ability of AI has evolved to the point where it can be another reflective brain of my own, and this brain can reason in a networked manner. It was also with its help that I discovered an interesting point of view, that is: the reason why you are easily distracted by various opportunities is because there is no main line. People without a main line see everything as opportunities; people with a main line only see a few opportunities worth taking seriously.

Yes, I thought that what I needed to build was a reusable and iterable system, but AI told me that I should confirm the main line before proceeding. The corresponding thing is: Tao is greater than Dharma, Tao comes first.

By the way, I saw that there were other kinds of chicken that were good, including Tyson’s chicken, which was also worth trying, so I bought a lot of food that I hadn’t planned on along the way. That bag is really heavy when you hold it up! As I walked home, I was glad that I didn’t choose manual labor to make a living...

Today I sorted out my diet and fitness work plan. This part is where I spend the most time and energy. Because it is quite complicated, involving the entire process from diet to exercise, and it is also the most energy-consuming. After finishing these parts, I had lost all my strength and energy. After going to the supermarket to buy groceries, I came back with a face full of exhaustion and tiredness. It was raining lightly outside at the moment, and the eaves were filled with pedestrians and tourists waiting for the rain to stop. And I walked into the rain without any scruples, heading towards the bus stop not far away...

After getting up this morning, I prepared lunch in advance, just to fix the recent frequent restart problem of my mobile phone. I decided to run to the Computer City at noon to review the cause. When I got on the bus, the sky was filled with dark clouds, which made me wonder when it would rain. As soon as we arrived at the station, the rain outside seemed to be pouring down and began to plop toward the ground. At this moment, even the middle-aged man holding an umbrella lowered his legs and feet on the rain shelter, waiting patiently for the rain to stop.

![City streets in heavy rain](/images/posts/1000096575.jpg)

At this moment, I was not frightened. I was just thinking about whether the computer in the backpack would be soaked by the rain and damaged due to my rash intrusion. After thinking for a moment and thinking that it was no big deal, I walked into the rain field decisively. Walking in the rain, I suddenly felt that there was nothing to fear, because it could only wet my clothes and backpack, but it could not dampen my determination to reach my destination. Walking in such a scene, I feel very lonely, and therefore my confidence in reaching my destination is gradually increasing. This reminds me of a sentence I just read in the Bible this morning: To everyone who has, to him who has, more will be given, so that he may have an excess; but from him who does not have, even what he has will be taken away from him.

Yes, whatever you are determined for, you will get more of. I chose to strengthen my determination, so when I saw the rain, it was no longer rain, because I was more worried about not being able to achieve my goal than being wet by the rain. With this kind of mentality, everything becomes much easier.

I am often tired, but also often motivated! The most common things along the way are all kinds of accidents and uncertainties. In such an environment, it actually strengthens a person's willpower and fighting spirit. If you persist, your world will be broader. Otherwise, you will be easily confused and lose the possibilities you should have...

Well, be careful and be alert!

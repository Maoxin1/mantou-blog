I seem to have more than my share of worries. Lately, I have been blowing ordinary little things out of proportion. I know how absurd that is and that it is not worth doing, but I keep doing it, almost as though I cannot stop. Eventually I felt I had to change, and realized how fragile I seemed in the face of it all.

If knowing myself better helps me make wiser choices, then I think I am more mature and clearer now than I have ever been.

How was today? Whatever it was like, it is over.

What will tomorrow bring? I do not know yet, but I will give it my all!

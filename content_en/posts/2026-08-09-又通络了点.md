Even now, I am using only a small fraction of what Git can do. If I understand it correctly, it could record the structure and details of every past iteration—a kind of electronic notebook. That makes Obsidian a good fit for something I really need: a place to keep revising my ideas.

An accidental experiment this afternoon showed me that I can import my earlier writing into one system and more clearly record how I understand concepts and the connections between them. I have not found a Git-style rollback there yet, but it gives me a visual map for thinking about the same idea or related ideas over time. Suddenly I felt alert again!

Now I can imagine organizing and iterating all my productive projects. My old Notion notes may naturally become a thing of the past. What I do next feels more meaningful.

Lately I have been running short of tokens. Thinking about it, that is probably a result of not planning well enough. What should I do? The same four words I keep coming back to: be prepared.

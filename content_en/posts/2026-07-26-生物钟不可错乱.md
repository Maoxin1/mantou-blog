I am so sleepy today!!! I can barely keep my eyes open. I wonder what going to work tomorrow will be like if I feel this way. It sounds like a disaster, so I had better go to bed early tonight.

The biggest challenge today may have been sitting patiently at my desk, directing my computer through some vibe coding for nearly five hours without stopping. It was quite an ordeal: I have been giving the computer a thorough checkup for three days now, and I still do not seem to be finished.

But it has also shown me how little I knew about my own computer. No wonder the very same machine can be so much more useful in one person’s hands than another’s. This has been a vivid little experiment in that difference...
